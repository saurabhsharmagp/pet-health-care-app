How it works

Three steps. No phone tag.

1

Pick a real open slot

Browse vets, walkers, and lab packages with live availability — the time you tap is the time you get, not a callback promise.

2

Confirm — that's it

No card details, no checkout flow while we finish onboarding professionals. Confirm the booking and settle up at the visit.

3

Manage it from one place

What's inside

Four ways Kennelo looks after your pet

Built as one connected app, not four separate bookings.

Vet Visits

In-clinic appointments with real vets, filtered by specialty and clinic distance, booked against their actual calendar.

Video Consults

Skip the trip for follow-ups and quick questions — face-to-face with your vet from wherever you and your pet are.

Lab Tests

Home sample collection for wellness panels, allergy screens, and senior checkups — results reviewed by a vet.

Dog Walking

Background-checked walkers for 30/60-minute walks, pet sitting, or overnight boarding — book down to the slot.
