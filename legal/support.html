Frequently asked questions

How do I book a vet appointment?

From the Home tab, tap Book Vet Visit, choose a vet, pick an available date and time, select the pet and reason for the visit, then confirm. You'll see the appointment under the Visits tab.

How do I book a dog walk?

Open the Walking tab, tap Find a Walker, choose a walker and an available slot, add the pickup address, and confirm the booking.

Do I need to pay in the app?

Not yet — online payment is coming soon. For now, bookings are confirmed without payment in‑app, and you settle up directly at the visit.

How do I message a vet or walker?

Go to the Consult tab (for vets) or open a booking's detail page, and use the chat icon to start or continue a conversation.

How do I cancel an appointment or walk?

Open the appointment or walk from the Visits or Walking tab and tap Cancel.

How do I delete my account or my data?

Email us at support@kennelo.com from the address on your account and we'll process your request. See our Privacy Policy for details on how your data is handled.

I'm a vet or dog walker — how do I get an account?

Vet and walker accounts are set up by the Kennelo team. Email us at support@kennelo.com with your details and we'll get you onboarded.

Still stuck?

Reach out at support@kennelo.com and let us know what device and app version you're using, along with a description of the issue — that helps us help you faster.
