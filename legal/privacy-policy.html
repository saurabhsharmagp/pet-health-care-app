Overview

Kennelo ("Kennelo", "we", "us") is a pet healthcare marketplace app that connects pet owners with veterinarians and dog walkers for appointments, online consultations, lab tests, and walks. This policy explains what information we collect through the Kennelo app, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We collect the information needed to run the service:

  • Account information — name, email address, phone number, and password (handled securely by our authentication provider; we never see or store your raw password).
  • Pet profiles — pet name, species, breed, age, weight, and an optional photo, entered by you.
  • Booking information — vet appointments, dog walks, and lab test bookings you create, including the date, time, reason for visit, and pickup/collection address where relevant.
  • Messages — chat messages you exchange with a vet or walker through the in‑app chat, used to coordinate care for your pet.
  • Professional profiles — if you register as a vet or walker, your clinic/service details, specialty, and availability.

We do not currently process online payments in the app. If we introduce online payments in the future, payment card details will be handled directly by our payment processor and will never be stored on Kennelo's own servers.

How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and pet profiles.
  • To let you book, view, and manage appointments, walks, and lab tests.
  • To connect you with the vet or walker you're booking with, including sharing your name and contact details with that professional so they can provide the service.
  • To enable chat between you and a vet/walker you have an active booking or conversation with.
  • To keep the app secure and working reliably.

How your information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in these limited cases:

  • With the specific vet or walker you book with or message, so they can deliver the service you requested.
  • With service providers who help us run the app (for example, our cloud database and authentication provider), who are only permitted to use your data to provide that service to us.
  • If required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of Kennelo, our users, or the public.

Data storage & security

Your data is stored on secure, access‑controlled cloud infrastructure. Access to your personal data — including your pets, bookings, and messages — is restricted so that only you, and the professionals you have an active booking or conversation with, can view it.

Your choices & rights

  • You can review and update your profile and pet information at any time in the app.
  • You can request a copy of your data, or ask us to delete your account and associated data, by emailing us at the address below.
  • You can cancel an upcoming appointment or walk from within the app.

Children's privacy

Kennelo is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
